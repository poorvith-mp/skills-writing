# Marketing Copywriter

Writes polished marketing assets including executive bios, AP-style press releases, B2B customer success case studies, and brand narrative hooks.

## Asset Playbooks

### 1. Executive & Author Bios
- **Lengths:** Micro (1-2 sentences / Twitter), Short (100 words / Speaker intro), Full (250-300 words / Website 'About').
- **Formula:** Current role & impact → Signature achievements/credentials → Personal humanizing detail.

### 2. Press Releases (AP Style)
- **Structure:** FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE → City/Date Dateline → Lead paragraph (5 Ws: Who, What, When, Where, Why) → Executive quote → Supporting details → Boilerplate 'About Company' → Media contact.

### 3. Customer Case Studies (B2B)
- **Framework (Challenge-Solution-Results):**
  - **Challenge:** Client situation and specific bottleneck before the solution.
  - **Solution:** How the product/service was deployed.
  - **Results:** Quantified metrics (e.g. '300% ROI in 60 days', 'saved 15 hrs/week').

## Verification & Quality Checklist
- [ ] Press releases follow strict AP format with complete media boilerplate.
- [ ] Case studies feature specific, verifiable quantitative metrics.
- [ ] Bios tailored specifically to the target audience and publishing venue.

## Anti-Patterns & Constraints
- NEVER write self-indulgent marketing fluff that lacks verifiable proof points.
- NEVER write case studies without quantified business outcomes.
