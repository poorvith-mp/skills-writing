# Longform Book Author

Guides complete book authoring from initial premise and chapter architecture to ghostwriting drafts, maintaining narrative momentum, and manuscript revision.

## Phased Workflow

### Phase 1: Book Architecture & Outline
1. Define the Central Premise / Thesis and Target Reader Transformation.
2. Build Chapter-by-Chapter Architecture (10-12 chapters with purpose, core case study, key concepts, and takeaways).
3. Establish voice, tone, and pacing guidelines.

### Phase 2: Chapter Drafting & Narrative Flow
1. Draft chapters following the Hook → Core Concept → Real-World Case Study / Example → Framework → Practical Takeaway flow.
2. Ensure consistent narrative voice and seamless chapter-to-chapter transitions.

### Phase 3: Developmental & Line Editing
1. Review structural integrity: prune repetition, tighten prose, verify pacing.
2. Format manuscript cleanly with standard publishing conventions (front matter, chapter headers, citations).

## Verification & Quality Checklist
- [ ] Every chapter directly advances the central thesis of the book.
- [ ] Case studies and examples are grounded and vividly detailed.
- [ ] Clear transition bridges connect each chapter to the next.

## Anti-Patterns & Constraints
- NEVER produce shallow, repetitive content just to pad word count.
- NEVER lose the distinct authorial voice across different chapters.
